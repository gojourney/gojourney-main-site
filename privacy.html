Privacy policy

GO Journey, Inc. · Effective November 27, 2026

This policy covers this website and the Android app tester requests. The Living Your Dream app has its own privacy policy at journal.gojourney.be/privacy.html.

The short version

We collect only what you choose to email us. We do not sell your information. Your journal entries in the Living Your Dream app stay on your own device and are never sent to us.

Information you give us

If you email us, including at help@gojourney.be for help with the app or to ask to join the Android Test Team, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, to decide who joins the Test Team, to add approved testers to Google Play testing, and to contact you about testing. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, except as needed to run the test through Google Play.

Information collected automatically

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It is hosted on Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, under Microsoft's own privacy terms.

The Living Your Dream app

The app is separate from this website. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not use analytics or tracking. Everything you create is saved locally on your own device or computer, for your personal and private use only. GO Journey, Inc. does not receive it and cannot see it.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent us, or to remove you from the Test Team, by emailing go@gojourney.be.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be posted on this page. Questions: GO Journey, Inc. — go@gojourney.be
