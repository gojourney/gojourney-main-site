Who we are

GO Journey, Inc. helps people of every age discover, declare and live their dreams. Our work centers on character formation across generations, mutual respect, and leadership as both a practical and a moral responsibility. Rooted in Appalachian and West Virginia community values, we build programs and tools that individuals can use at home, in school and at work.

Our leader

Melissa J. Hill, M.S.Ed., leads GO Journey, Inc. She holds a master's degree in Adult and Technical Education (minor in Management Information Systems) and a bachelor's degree in Business Administration (Marketing), with 38 years of professional experience. A proven grant writer and program manager, published author and new AI app innovator, she was born, raised and trained up in Chesapeake, West Virginia.

What we offer

  • Living Your Dream – GO Journey: a guided journal and life planner for individuals. Learn more about the app.
  • The GO Journey model: a character and career development framework, including the KnowMe Index and the Occupational Maturity Journey.
  • Discover and Declare Your Dream: a booklet that helps you put your dream into words.
