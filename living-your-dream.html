The story in the line

The Dream Ping is where you decide if you go from workforce to living — in active pursuit. So many dismiss or don’t have the courage to learn and take the leap.

  1. 1Learning and WorkingAlmost a flat line. Working for another, gaining experience in the workforce or from job to job.
  2. 2Dream PingA small blip on the line. You see your dream.
  3. 3The leapYou take the leap. Not taking the leap results in continued workforce.
  4. 4Ground zeroThe leap, or a termination, takes you to rock bottom.
  5. 5The climbThe only way back up is to get up. Work, sometimes for less, to learn.
  6. 6Enroute to “there”Getting experience and making the climb to there.
  7. 7In Active PursuitEvery individual building wealth for self and posterity.
The only way to transition into the new workforce and remain employable is a personal and professional maturity plan that aligns you with Education & Workforce 4.0 in preparation for living in Society 5.0. An intra/entrepreneurial training up as an employee or business owner is required to be employable.

The program

Living Your Dream walks you through six steps, from discovering your dream to celebrating it.

  • KnowLearn who you are, including your strengths, needs, personality and wellness.
  • BelieveBuild the confidence that your dream is worth pursuing.
  • DeclareWrite, sign and date your personal dream statement.
  • PlanSet goals, steps and a life mission.
  • JourneyTake the steps and track your progress.
  • CelebrateMark your wins and thank the people who helped you.

The app: three steps, three separate modules

The Living Your Dream app is a guided journal and life planner for individuals. A simple path from “Who am I?” to a dream you have signed and are living. Start with step 1. Everything you write saves on your own device, and you can move between steps any time.

What is inside

  • Declare Your Dream: your own signed dream statement.
  • My JourneyBoard: a progress dashboard across 15 dimensions of a whole life.
  • KnowMe Index: self-discovery covering wellness, needs, personality and strengths.
  • Occupational Maturity Journey: see your next career stage, from Labor to Entrepreneur.
  • Into Tech: find the technology path that fits your life and experience, whether you are a student who loves computers and gaming, a parent or caregiver, or an experienced worker.
  • GO Journey Plan: goals, projects and tasks. Plus a daily journal, wellness log and challenges.
  • My JourneyMap: the places that shaped your story. Case studies for real-life situations.
  • Listen: have any page or section read aloud. The app also works offline.

Built for every starting point

This app gives credit for life experience, whether you are young or older, and never penalizes anyone for caring for themselves or their family. It is for students, parents, caregivers, working adults, people between jobs, and people starting over.

Private by design

Your journal is saved on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and the app works offline. Everything you create is saved locally to your own personal device or computer, for your personal and private use only. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
